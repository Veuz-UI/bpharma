About BPharma

Committed to the quality of laboratory products

BPharma seeks to contribute to the field of medicine through acquiring and implementing new technologies that are in line with the European standards of EU-GMP and EU-DRALEX. Through our facility located in Saudi Arabia, our vision is to manufacture and produce distinct pharmaceutical products in various forms that are not available in the MENA region, accommodating the requirements of medical institutions and end users alike.

Our focus is to cover a variety of therapeutic classes and meet growing demand in both the domestic and international markets, securing the availability of important life-saving products, creating new jobs, and training opportunities.
